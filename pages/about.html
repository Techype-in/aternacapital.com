Our story

Built to last.
Built to listen.

Aterna comes from Aeternum — Latin for eternal. It is not just a name. It is the commitment we make to every borrower we work with.

Why we exist

Credit that sees
the whole picture

Too much credit in India is transactional. A business applies, a score is generated, a product is dispensed. The nuance of how that business actually operates — its cycles, its relationships, its ambitions — rarely enters the picture.

"The best financing structures come from spending time with a business — not processing it through a system."

Aterna Capital was founded to change that. We are an NBFC built on the belief that credit should be deliberate, lasting, and deeply customised.

Our approach

Relationship
before transaction

Before we structure a deal, we understand a business. Before we price a loan, we study a sector. Before we disburse, we know your name — not just your CIN.

This takes more time. We believe it is worth it — for us, and far more so for you. Our portfolio reflects this. We hold fewer, deeper relationships.
