Our belief

Credit built on
conviction.

Too much credit in India is transactional. A business applies, a score is generated, a product is dispensed. The nuance of how a business operates rarely enters the picture.

"We invest time understanding your business before we invest capital in it."

Aterna was built to change that — an NBFC that believes the best financing structures come from spending time with a business, not processing it through a system.

01
Permanence

Long-arc relationships — growing alongside borrowers through cycles, not exiting at the first sign of complexity.

02
Deliberate underwriting

Every deal is studied, not just scored. Sector knowledge and business model understanding matter as much as the P&L.

03
Custom fit

No off-the-shelf products. Every structure is designed around your cash flow, your sector, and your timeline.

04
Accessibility

Premium, thoughtful credit should not be the exclusive preserve of large corporations. Ambition deserves the same quality of thinking at every scale.
